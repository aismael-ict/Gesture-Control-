=============================================================================
                          HAND CONTROL NEXT-GEN 2.0
=============================================================================

Welcome to Hand Control Next-Gen 2.0: an advanced, touchless AI-powered 
gesture control platform with Air Mouse, dynamic motion swipes, multi-hand 
tracking, and low-latency Windows system integration.

QUICK START
-----------
1. Run `run_nextgen.bat` or execute `python hand_control_nextgen.py`.
2. A preview window opens showing the camera feed with the futuristic neon
   hand skeleton and HUD.
3. Bring your hand into view. Click the application you want to control once.

HOTKEYS (IN PREVIEW WINDOW)
---------------------------
  M .......... Toggle Air Mouse mode (touchless cursor control)
  P .......... Pause / Resume gesture tracking
  D .......... Toggle Dark Cyber Canvas (hides room background)
  H .......... Toggle HUD overlays (minimal camera view)
  Q or Esc ... Quit cleanly

GESTURES & CAPABILITIES
-----------------------

1. AIR MOUSE MODE (Press 'M' to toggle)
   - Move: Point with your Index fingertip inside the active interaction box.
     Smooth 1€ (One-Euro) filtering eliminates all tremor and jitter.
   - Left Click: Pinch Index finger to Thumb tip.
   - Drag & Drop: Hold the Index+Thumb pinch and move your hand.
   - Right Click: Pinch Middle finger to Thumb tip.
   - Air Scroll: Extend Index and Middle fingers together and move up/down.

2. MEDIA & VOLUME GESTURES
   - Thumbs Up ................. Volume Up (repeats while held)
   - Thumbs Down ............... Volume Down (repeats while held)
   - Fist (curled tight) ....... Mute / Unmute audio

3. NAVIGATION & PRESENTATION GESTURES
   - Point Right (Index) ....... Right Arrow / Next Slide / Fast-forward
   - Point Left (Index) ........ Left Arrow / Previous Slide / Rewind
   - Pinch (Index + Thumb) ..... Spacebar (Play/Pause / Confirm)

4. POWER USER SHORTCUTS
   - Peace / V-Sign (Index + Middle straight) .. Screenshot (Win + Shift + S)
   - Rock On (Index + Pinky straight) ......... Toggle Fullscreen (F11)
   - Open Palm ("Stop" 5 fingers open) ........ Freeze pointer / Neutral pose

5. DYNAMIC SWIPES (Fast hand motion)
   - Swipe Left / Right ........ Switch Active App (Alt + Tab)
   - Swipe Down ................ Minimize All / Show Desktop (Win + D)

MULTI-HAND CAPABILITY
---------------------
Tracks up to 2 hands simultaneously. You can use your Left hand for Volume and
Media while using your Right hand for Air Mouse navigation!

CUSTOMIZATION (config.json)
---------------------------
All thresholds, sensitivities, audio feedback settings, and key mappings are
stored in `config.json`. You can modify `config.json` to customize keybindings
for YouTube, PowerPoint, VLC, games, or web browsing.

PRIVACY & PERFORMANCE
---------------------
- 100% on-device local execution using Google MediaPipe Hand Landmarker.
- No network requests, no telemetry, and no video frames are ever recorded or sent.
